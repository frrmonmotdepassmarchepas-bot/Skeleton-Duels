# Skeleton-Duels

A retro 2D RPG boss-fight minigame written in pure C# (Windows Forms), inspired by classic turn-based bullet-hell battles.

## IMPORTANT
* **Do not touch or delete** `compiler.bat` or `game.cs`.
* Double-click **`Skeleton Duel.exe`** to launch the game.

## How to Play
* **Fight, Act, or Heal:** Choose your action each turn to defeat the Skeleton or befriend him.
* **Dodge:** Use the **Arrow Keys** to move your soul inside the box during enemy attacks.
* **Settings:** Adjust the **music volume** or switch the language between **English** and **French** directly from the main menu.
* **Quit:** Press **`ESC`** at any time to exit fullscreen.

*(Note: The character design is original and intentionally distinct from Sans in Undertale).*
